# Roster Sprint Film

## Objective

Deliver a 9.6-second, social-first launch punch that communicates one transformation: `200 tools → Roster focus → Starting Five`. It should feel fast, elastic, clean, and readable on the first muted viewing.

## Story spine

1. **Impact (0.0–1.3s):** `200` lands immediately; five bars answer it.
2. **Compression (1.1–3.1s):** five large capabilities rush through one Roster gateway; `200 → 1` becomes physical.
3. **Selection (2.8–5.2s):** one cobalt scan resolves the five; rejected schema strokes leave in the same leftward current.
4. **Lineup (4.9–7.4s):** five hero tools lock on successive impacts, followed by one short dramatic hold.
5. **Identity (7.1–9.6s):** the formation accelerates upward into the Roster mark; tagline and command hold cleanly.

## Art direction

Use paper white, graphite, cobalt, natural green, and warm amber only. Scale comes from type, five large glass objects, and thick curved paths. There are no particle fields, tiny sprites, decorative HUD labels, purple/turquoise ambience, or overlapping scene layers.

## Motion and audio

The dominant current travels left. Every seam cuts during motion and carries either the five bars, a thick path, or the hero cards into the next beat. Entries use short back-out settles rather than elastic wobble. The original local score targets roughly 150 BPM with five lineup impacts, a brief pre-reveal vacuum, and a clean final hit.
