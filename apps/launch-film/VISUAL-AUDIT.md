# Visual Audit

## Preserve

- The 57-second, eleven-scene narrative is clear and factually careful.
- The first-three-second `200 tools / five start` hook reads quickly.
- Terminal typing, deterministic tool data, centralized copy, audio cues, composition registration, and QA tooling are sound production foundations.
- The overload-to-selection contrast, suggestion-only Sixth Man behavior, and pre-season League state communicate the right product story.

## Redesign

- The near-black HUD palette makes every scene feel like the same technical dashboard. The redesign moves to luminous warm white, soft graphite type, translucent mineral glass, and controlled spectral accents.
- Tool cards are too small and repetitive. Replace them with three object classes: abstract background capsules, readable candidates, and large Starting Five hero slabs.
- The atom-like Roster core is generic and overused. Replace it with a recognizable five-aperture glass lens that changes state without changing identity.
- Hairline dotted connections disappear at social-feed scale. Replace them with broad refractive ribbons and high-contrast request/result lanes.
- Scene compositions overuse a centered radial node. Introduce left-to-right traversal, deep field perspectives, layered stage formations, and asymmetric negative space.
- Terminal, Coach, and League surfaces need fewer labels, larger text, stronger material depth, and clearer focal hierarchy.

## Delete

- Persistent scene chrome, tiny scene numbers, corner captions, micro progress bars, decorative brackets, scanner ticks, dust sprites, generic plus signs, and repeated green fog pools.
- Candidate metadata that cannot be read at 960×540.
- Decorative notches and one-pixel outlines that make every object feel like the same HUD card.
- Repeated concentric rings that do not encode a Roster state.

## Simplify

- Show at most five detailed tools; imply scale with low-detail depth objects and grouped capability bands.
- Reduce each supporting message to one short line.
- Coach becomes one local learning curve; League becomes one evidence-gated ranking card.
- Search evaluation uses three large signals—fit, reliability, local history—revealed sequentially.

## Motion Refinement

- Keep frame-derived deterministic motion and scene timing, but replace uniform linear entrances with curved depth trajectories and staged internal reveals.
- Let the camera make slow 50–140-frame moves while hero objects complete 24–45-frame arrivals.
- Connections draw only after objects settle. Fast foreground passes and rejection fragments receive directional blur; text remains crisp.
- Preserve spatial handoffs between terminal, overload, search, clearing, lineup, and final mark, while limiting each moment to three competing motion systems.

## Before-State Evidence

The original master, preview, contact sheet, poster, and 33-frame QA matrix remain in `out/` as the immutable comparison baseline. Redesign outputs are written to `out/redesign/`.
