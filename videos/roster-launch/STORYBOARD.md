## Video direction

- **Palette:** warm cream `#fdfae7`, ink `#111111`, cobalt `#1e2bfa`; green and red appear only for truthful success/failure state. No cyan, purple, or multicolour glow.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk for display and numerals, Manrope for supporting copy, JetBrains Mono for terminal text. Load-bearing type stays above 28 px at 1920×1080.
- **Objects:** background tools are unlabeled capsules; candidates carry one glyph, one title, and one capability; starters become substantial hero objects with a single supporting line.
- **Motion grammar:** sharp anticipation, critically damped recoil, small one-shot overshoot on hero arrivals, delayed internal content, and broad camera movement. No idle wobble, elastic loops, or motion for decoration.
- **Continuity:** a leftward spatial current crosses ordinary seams. The Starting Five and final identity receive the only inverse zoom-through arrivals. Every scene ends with a held, legible shape that the next scene inherits.
- **Audio:** original 126 BPM tactile score with rubbery low end, dry plucks, terminal ticks, scan whooshes, lineup impacts, one short failure glitch, and a warm final chord. No narration and no stock corporate uplift.
- **Negative list:** no HUD chrome, tiny metadata, random particles, decorative grids, thin dotted routes, repeated central orb, broken overlap, or unsupported operational claims.
