# Initial visual audit

## Preserve

- The existing Roster narrative and 11-beat timing are strong and remain intact.
- Terminal typing, local-first framing, Sixth Man truthfulness and pre-season League labelling remain explicit.
- Deterministic data and a replaceable audio bed are retained.

## Redesign

- Replaced repeated central-orb/HUD-card compositions with a paper-white editorial field, a distinctive five-aperture prism and asymmetric hero lineup.
- Rebuilt tool communication into three visual scales: capsules for implied scale, readable candidate objects for evaluation, and large hero cards for the Starting Five.
- Increased typography hierarchy and safe margins; supporting copy is now sparse and social-readable.
- Re-authored motion around staged entrances, curved ribbons, a moving search beam, card-to-fragment clearing and a deliberate final convergence.

## Delete

- No persistent top/bottom chrome, random tiny sprites, decorative brackets, repeated technical ticks, hairline dotted networks or miniature progress grids are present in the production path.

## Simplify

- Coach/League is one learning chart plus one explicitly pre-season card.
- Tool overload uses eight readable candidates plus a deterministic capsule field rather than dozens of detailed cards.
- Sixth Man uses one failure card and one suggestion card; it never implies silent automatic substitution.

## Motion-only refinement

- The strongest existing timing beats were preserved while changing the motion grammar to use slower camera-scale changes, short object settles, and sparse colour signals. The canvas draw loop is tied to Diffusion Studio’s ticker so scrub, capture and render share one clock.

## Second-pass QA decisions

- The production source is now `src/roster-film-final.jsx`, a clean canvas-first rebuild rather than a layer over the earlier pass.
- The universe request is a dark contrast anchor; terminal escape chips leave the terminal bounds into negative space; the final lineup exits before the wordmark lockup holds.
- Sound is generated by `scripts/make-sound-design.mjs` with deterministic cues for every major beat. The old bed remains only as a reference asset and is not mounted by the final composition.
