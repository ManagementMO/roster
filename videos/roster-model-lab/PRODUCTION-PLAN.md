## Product and identity rules

Roster is a local MCP tool router for AI agents. Transparent passthrough is the default. Optional five mode returns a task-specific shortlist of up to five capabilities by default. The agent chooses the call; Roster routes it; the backend returns a result. Coach records derived outcomes locally for future routing. Backend tools may use networks. The film is an illustrative workflow, not a recording of a shipped dashboard. A returned snapshot does not mean a bug was fixed.

Preserve the exact approved slender five-part R with longer legs. Do not thicken, shorten, or redraw it. Palette: pearl, navy, cobalt, with restrained pale-blue light. Vendor marks retain their natural colors; no repeated vendor in a scene. Modern flat/two-tone icons and pearl cards. Avoid orange backgrounds, neon-on-black tiles, dated bevels, random colors, and generic particle clouds. Closing URL: github.com/ManagementMO/roster. Do not restore the removed release-status caption or invent install commands, release dates, or benchmarks.

Keep text readable and exact; final identity holds at least two seconds. Preserve raw model results separately from finished cuts. Essential text and logos can be composited from original assets when the model distorts them. Describe native frame rate honestly; any upscaling/interpolation must be labeled. Do not replace the existing approved film.
