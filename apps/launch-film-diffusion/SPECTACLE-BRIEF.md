# Roster Launch Film — Spectacle Pass

## Creative thesis

Roster turns an impossible tool universe into a decisive lineup. The film should perform that transformation physically: typography expands until it becomes space, tool traffic twists into a tunnel, Roster focuses the tunnel into five material objects, and those objects resolve into the brand mark. Every transition inherits a shape, direction, or momentum from the previous shot.

## Viewer experience

The first three seconds are loud, legible and surprising. The middle alternates between dense camera movement and quiet focus. The Starting Five is the visual and musical payoff. The Sixth Man interrupts that confidence for less than a second before restoring control. The final mark holds long enough to read and replay.

## Art direction

- Editorial white and near-black chapters create contrast across the timeline.
- Graphite, cobalt, sky blue, natural green, warm amber and failure red provide clean pop without synthetic turquoise or purple ambience.
- Glass is an optical event: lens highlights, edge energy and responsive tint. It is reserved for hero objects.
- Background scale comes from ribbons, planes, type and a small set of unlabeled depth tokens—not tiny decorative sprites.
- Typography behaves as an object: it masks, pushes, splits, wipes and becomes geometry.
- One focal object owns each frame; supporting structure recedes.

## Motion grammar

- Anticipation precedes every hero move.
- Fast actions overshoot and settle; slow camera moves use broad ease-in/ease-out curves.
- Secondary details follow the parent by 3–10 frames.
- Match cuts happen at peak velocity so scene boundaries feel continuous.
- The film alternates `compression → release`, `wide → macro`, and `white → ink`.
- Continuous ambient movement never exceeds two supporting systems at once.

## Audio arc

An original 112 BPM score combines a warm detuned pad, syncopated bass, tactile percussion, stereo air and authored scene effects. The score builds across the whole film rather than resetting at every scene. Search adds an ascending motif; clearing introduces granular rhythm; five tuned impacts land the lineup; the Sixth Man briefly removes the low end; the final identity resolves into a wide chord and sub release.

## Truth boundaries

- `npx roster init` is the launch command shown.
- Roster is local-first and shown as one local endpoint.
- Sixth Man is explicitly suggestion-only and requires agent acceptance.
- Coach shows local outcome learning.
- League is labelled pre-season with no certified rank claim.

## Acceptance bar

The pass is complete only when the master has a clear focal point at every sampled frame, no collisions or clipped type, at least one meaningful visual development every five seconds, continuous scene-to-scene momentum, a readable muted narrative, a non-generic audio crescendo, and clean 1080p H.264/AAC output.
