# Launch music

The current launch cut uses **Techno Fest Vibes** by Alejandro Magaña (A. M.),
downloaded from Mixkit's free music library. It replaces the quieter Minimal
Techno 01 bed with a more propulsive EDM/tech-house pulse suited to the 12.4s
product-launch cut:

<https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/tag/technology/>

Source file: `mixkit-techno-fest-vibes.mp3` (Mixkit asset `124.mp3`). Mixkit
describes its music library as free for commercial and personal projects under
the [Mixkit Music License](https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree). Keep this
note with the asset if the film is distributed outside the repository.

The render pipeline trims the track to 12.4 seconds and mixes it with the
curated real recordings in `assets/sfx/mixkit/`, assembled by
`scripts/make-real-sfx-bed.mjs` into `assets/roster-launch-sfx.wav`.
