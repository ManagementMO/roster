The current render, timing and media evidence is in each production's `verification/identity-adoption.json` and `VISUAL-QA.md`. Those files describe local production checks; they do not establish a product release or public website deployment.

- [Launch covers](../assets/brand/roster-launch-cover/README.md): “200 tools. Your starting five.” in landscape and portrait.
- [Canonical logo kit](../assets/brand/roster-r-01/README.md): approved SVGs, outlined wordmarks, transparent PNGs and icons.
- The website source lives in `apps/site/` on the website branch until that branch is integrated.

The other video directories and the `apps/launch-film*` workspaces preserve earlier production experiments. Their source is retained for reference; the two editions above are the current launch deliverables. Local caches, generated preview frames and rollback directories are intentionally excluded from Git.
