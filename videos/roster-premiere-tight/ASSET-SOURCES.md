# Asset sources for the 15-second revision

## Current soundtrack — Pocket Groove, September 8

The current bed is HeyGen music catalog item `42cc02db157645af90a944d8bd53e77a`, retrieved through the authenticated `heygen audio sounds list` catalog. The provider describes an upbeat jazzy instrumental with Rhodes keys, upright bass, brushed drums and muted horns. No artist credit was surfaced in its response. “Pocket Groove” is the working title for our edit, not an authorship claim over that recording. A 7.704–23.704s excerpt is frozen losslessly in `assets/audio/pocket-groove-source.flac`, with its digest and treatment in `verification/pocket-groove-mix.json`. The full retrieved recording remains in the local `.media` cache. Expiring signed download URLs and credentials are not persisted in public source notes.

The 37 picture-timed effects are original synthesis in `scripts/mix-pocket-groove.py`. The master and listening copies are `pocket-groove-final.wav` and `roster-pocket-groove.m4a`; the widescreen and feed editions use identical copies of these audio assets. All references to Full Send below describe the preceding audio revision.

## Artwork and earlier audio provenance

- **Roster mark:** the owner-approved slim five-part R, with extended lower legs, copied from `../../assets/brand/roster-r-01/roster-mark-pearl.svg`. The closing animation uses identical native paths with scene-prefixed IDs. Its standalone outlined wordmark is extracted from the pearl lockup without changing letter geometry. The kit documents image-generation exploration and the separately refined vector source. The legacy raster remains archived; no active scene uses it.
- **GitHub, Linear and Playwright marks:** existing SVG assets with provenance in the original production. Playwright retains the complete original red/green artwork. `linear-color.svg` preserves the existing Simple Icons geometry with Linear's `#5E6AD2` brand fill, also recorded in the earlier production's design tokens. `github-light.svg` preserves the GitHub geometry with an ivory fill for dark surfaces. No color filter or mask is applied during rendering. These identify illustrative capabilities; they do not imply endorsement.
- **Figma mark:** the complete multicolor SVG resolved from [SVGL's Figma asset](https://svgl.app/library/figma.svg), frozen as `.media/images/logo_001.svg` and copied unchanged to `assets/brands/figma.svg`. Its provider and source URL are recorded in `.media/manifest.jsonl`. It replaces one repeated GitHub tile in the opening collection.
- **Capability icon set:** custom vector illustrations drawn in the HTML on a shared 44×44 grid. The lineup uses flat two-tone blue/gold/teal/violet/indigo icons on soft white cards, without bevels, gradients or shine strokes. The snapshot and local-storage glyphs are also drawn SVG. The database retains teal-blue shading and a mint top surface; signal chips use semantic green/coral/amber colors. These are original illustration treatments, not third-party brand marks.
- **Fonts:** local Space Grotesk, Manrope and JetBrains Mono variable WOFF2 files. Their included SIL OFL license texts are preserved.
- **GSAP:** the existing local runtime, so no font or animation CDN is needed during rendering.
- **Music and sound effects:** **Five in Motion — Full Send**, an original pop-rock composition and arrangement for this exact 15-second cut. Notes, rhythms, amp processing, stereo mix and original interface effects are in `scripts/compose-pop-rock.py`. Individual recorded instrument samples come from Karoryfer's [Black And Green Guitars](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars), [Black And Blue Basses](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses) and [Big Rusty Drums](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums). The publisher lists these free libraries as [CC0](https://shop.karoryfer.com/pages/free-samples). The 96-file sample palette, three license texts and source/hash manifest are frozen in `assets/audio/samples/`. No existing song or stock music loop is used. The earlier electronic score and glossy icon scene are preserved under `verification/before-pop-rock/`.
- **Earlier audio:** the copied `.media/` records retain provenance from the first production. The prior Mixkit and HeyGen audio is not included in the revised soundtrack.

The five audio stems, PCM master, AAC listening copy and composition event list are included. See `MUSIC.md` for the arrangement and reproduction commands.

The final Playwright landing tap is an original short synthesis in `landing_tap()` within `scripts/compose-pop-rock.py`, with a separate fixed seed, a 660Hz decaying body and a brief filtered texture. It uses no additional external sample. The 4:5 edition copies these same original brand vectors, fonts, Roster artwork and final audio master byte-for-byte; its provenance points back to this production.

The earlier pearl-and-cobalt revision changed surrounding materials, type colors and transitions only. Existing vendor SVGs, the Roster mark, the five capability illustrations, the colored database and the music master remain preserved. The Playwright transition reuses the existing original SVG as a CSS image, with one visible instance throughout the handoff.
